Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Suxam AI ("the App", "we", "us") collects when you use it, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using Suxam AI, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Email address — collected when you sign in, either by typing it manually or through "Continue with Google". This is used to identify you within the App and is not used for marketing.
  • Messages you send — the questions and messages you type to the AI assistant are processed to generate a response, and are stored so the App can recognize your history.
  • Images you upload or generate — photos you attach for editing, and images you ask the App to generate, are sent to our AI service provider to produce a result.
  • Documents you upload — text or PDF files you attach are read to answer your questions about them.
  • Basic usage data — such as how many messages you've sent, to operate free-tier limits and improve the App.

We do not knowingly collect payment information, government ID numbers, precise real-time location, or health information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core features of the App: chat, image generation, image editing, and document Q&A.
  • To recognize you across visits so you don't need to sign in repeatedly.
  • To allow the App's administrators to view usage activity (such as questions asked) for support, moderation, and improving the service.
  • To enforce free-tier usage limits and manage premium access.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To provide its features, the App sends data to the following third-party service providers, who process it under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Sign-In — used only to let you sign in with your Google account; we receive your email address from Google, nothing else.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI — processes your messages, uploaded images, and documents to generate the App's responses.
  • DuckDuckGo — when you use the "web search" feature, your search query is sent to DuckDuckGo to fetch results.
  • Piston (code execution API) — when you use the "Run" feature on code, the code is sent to this third-party service to execute and return output.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your email address and message history are stored in our backend database (Cloudflare KV) and are retained until you request deletion. Uploaded images and documents are processed to generate a response and are not permanently stored beyond what's needed to answer your request.

5. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can stop using the App at any time.
  • You can request that we delete your stored email and message history by contacting us (see below).
  • If you signed in with Google, you can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account connections page.

6. Children's Privacy

Suxam AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
